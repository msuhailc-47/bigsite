# Site Details — Dorek International Enterprises LLP

## Project Overview
This website was built for **Dorek International Enterprises LLP**, styled as a premium, modern **Corporate Conglomerate Website** representing a parent company with multiple brands (including *Doorcarts*, *Doorcarts My Store*, *Associates*, and various operational divisions).

---

## 1. Core Brand Identity & Tagline
- **Company Name**: Dorek International Enterprises LLP
- **Tagline**: *"Think Better... Work Better... Grow Together..."*
- **Visual Design**: Dark-mode corporate design system with glassmorphism, gold & emerald accents, smooth hover transitions, and animated gradient effects.
- **Localization**: Full bilingual support for **English (EN)** and **Malayalam (ML)** with an instant language toggle in the header.

---

## 2. Web Page Structure & Features

### 1. Home / Hero Section
- Full-viewport height banner with floating background particles and animated gradient effects.
- Prominent Dorek logo & gold subtitle.
- Core Tagline & introductory description.
- Action Buttons: `Get Started` and `Contact Us`.
- Animated scroll-down indicator.

### 2. About Dorek
- Company History & background.
- **Vision & Mission** cards.
- **6 Core Values**: Integrity & Trust, Innovation & Excellence, Customer First, Teamwork & Growth, Social Responsibility, Sustainable Development.
- **Founder's Message**: Styled glassmorphism quote block.
- **Company Timeline**: Interactive timeline tracing company milestones from incorporation (2020) to pan-India vision (2025).

### 3. Business Divisions (`Our Businesses`)
- Grid layout showcasing 8 key company divisions:
  1. **Doorcarts** (Flagship Retail & Wholesale Brand)
  2. **Doorcarts My Store** (Franchise Model)
  3. **Retail Network** (Branded Outlets)
  4. **Distribution Division** (Multi-channel Wholesale)
  5. **Service Division** (Installation & Repairs)
  6. **Maintenance Division** (Annual Maintenance Contracts - AMC)
  7. **Training Division** (Skill Development)
  8. **Software Division** (Enterprise Tech Solutions)

### 4. Why Choose Dorek
- 7 Value Propositions with custom icons and numbered highlights:
  1. Complete Engineering Solutions Under One Roof
  2. High-Quality Products from Trusted Brands
  3. Professional Installation & Technical Support
  4. Annual Maintenance & After-Sales Service
  5. Customized Solutions for Residential, Commercial & Industrial Projects
  6. Experienced Engineering Team
  7. Competitive Pricing & Reliable Service

### 5. Products & Services
A multi-category tabbed catalog covering **10 major engineering & product sectors**:
1. **Electrical Solutions** (Wires, Switches, MCBs, Panels, Conduits)
2. **Plumbing Solutions** (Pipes, CP & Sanitary, Valves, Water Supply)
3. **Hardware & Building Materials** (Tools, Safety Gear, Fasteners)
4. **Pumps & Water Systems** (Domestic, Industrial, Booster & Submersible Pumps)
5. **Lighting Solutions** (LED, Decorative, Street, Flood & Smart Lights)
6. **Solar Energy Solutions** (Panels, Inverters, On/Off-Grid & Hybrid Power Plants, Solar EPC)
7. **Inverter & Battery Solutions** (Home & Commercial UPS, Lithium/Tubular Batteries)
8. **Water Treatment Solutions** (Domestic/Commercial RO, Water Softeners, Sewage Treatment)
9. **Building Automation & Smart Solutions** (Smart Home, CCTV, Access Control, Biometrics)
10. **Engineering & Technical Services** (Electrical & Plumbing Contracting, Energy Audits, AMC)

### 6. Business Opportunities
- 8 partnership models for entrepreneurs, dealers, and investors:
  - *Associate*, *Franchise*, *Authorized Outlet*, *Investor*, *Silent Partner*, *Active Partner*, *Dealer*, *Distributor*.
- `Apply Now` button triggering the Portal Login / Application modal.

### 7. Software Division
- Interactive grid of 7 software products:
  - **ERP System**, **CRM Platform**, **Billing Software**, **Inventory Management**, **Accounts & Finance**, **Biometric Attendance**, **Mobile Apps**.
- Custom CSS dashboard mockups for each software product.

### 8. Network Across Kerala
- **Interactive SVG Map of Kerala**: Rendered using official GeoJSON district vectors for all 14 districts.
- Interactive hover tooltips showing Hubs, Outlets, and Associates per district.
- **Animated Statistics Bar**: 14 Districts, 50+ Hubs, 200+ Outlets, 500+ Associates.

### 9. Investors & Careers
- Investor relations info (Investment models, shareholding, profit sharing, growth plan).
- Careers hub with tabbed navigation: *Current Openings*, *Internships*, and *Training Programs*.

### 10. Media, Downloads & CSR
- **News & Events**: Corporate announcements & events.
- **Media Gallery**: Tabbed photos, videos, and achievements gallery.
- **Download Center**: Access corporate brochures, catalogs, and forms (PDF format).
- **CSR / Social Responsibility**: Skill development, community welfare, and employment generation initiatives.

### 11. Contact Hub & Footer
- Detailed contact details (Address, Phone, Email, WhatsApp).
- Inquiry form with dropdown selection for business types.
- Corporate footer with quick links, legal policies, back-to-top button, and social media icons.

### 12. Interactive Features
- **Portal Login Modal**: Popup for Member, Outlet, ERP, Customer, and Investor logins.
- **AI Chat Assistant**: Floating assistant widget with automated response flows.

---

## 3. Technology Stack & Deployment Setup
- **Framework**: Vite + React
- **Icons**: `lucide-react` & Custom SVGs
- **Styling**: Pure CSS3 with variables, glassmorphism design, responsive grid & flexbox
- **Map Vector Engine**: `d3-geo` projection of Kerala GeoJSON boundaries
- **Deployment Setup**:
  - GitHub Repository: `https://github.com/msuhailc-47/app-dorek`
  - GitHub Pages Base Path: `/app-dorek/` configured in `vite.config.js`
  - Deployment scripts (`predeploy` & `deploy`) configured with `gh-pages`.
